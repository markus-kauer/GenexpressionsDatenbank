About This Website

What is the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)?

The Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) is a public database managed by the National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) to store gene expression data. Researchers worldwide contribute datasets, making GEO a critical resource for analyzing biological processes.



The Importance of Gene Expression Analysis

Gene expression analysis helps researchers understand gene regulation across tissues and conditions. These insights are essential for biomedical research and the development of new therapies.



Core GEO Data Types:

  • GPL (Platform): Specific technologies used to measure gene expression, such as platform GPL570. Each platform provides details on the probes and methods used.
  • GSE (Series): A collection of related experiments submitted by researchers, often covering multiple samples under different conditions.
  • GSM (Sample): Individual samples within a study, representing specific tissue or cell states.


Data Features:

  • Normalization Methods: Choose from various normalization techniques like quantile normalization or Z-score transformation to prepare data for reliable analysis.
  • Data Visualization: Generate boxplots to compare gene expression patterns across different conditions and samples.
  • Data Downloads: Export data in CSV format for further analysis.
